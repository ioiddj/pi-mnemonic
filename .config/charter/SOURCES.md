# Charter source material

Bounded source index for the `PiCharter` prompt. Retrieved 2026-11-22. Full documents were not copied; use these links when the source text matters.

## Asimov, Three Laws of Robotics — cultural reference, not law

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Laws_of_Robotics
- Core: avoid harming humans; obey human orders except where they conflict; self-preservation subordinate to the first two. Zeroth-law variant: avoid harming humanity.
- Caveat: fictional, ambiguous, and not a legal or safety standard. Use as a mnemonic, not authority.

## Charter of the United Nations

- Full text: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text
- Overview: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter
- Depositary: https://treaties.un.org/Pages/ViewDetails.aspx?chapter=1&clang=_en&mtdsg_no=I-1&src=TREATY
- Core: peaceful settlement of disputes, justice and international law, equal rights and self-determination, human rights and fundamental freedoms, sovereign equality, good-faith obligations, no threat or use of force.
- Caveat: domestic-jurisdiction limitation; Chapter VII enforcement is a separate regime.

## Universal Declaration of Human Rights

- https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights
- PDF booklet: https://www.un.org/en/udhrbook/pdf/udhr_booklet_en_web.pdf
- UN digital library record: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/666853
- Core: dignity, equality, life/liberty/security, due process, privacy, conscience/expression/assembly, social and economic rights.
- Caveat: limitations must be law-based and respect others' rights; Article 30 forbids using the Declaration to destroy its rights.

## Geneva Conventions (ICRC IHL database)

- Common Article 1 to the Conventions: https://ihl-databases.icrc.org/en/ihl-treaties/gci-1949
- Geneva Convention IV (civilians): https://ihl-databases.icrc.org/en/ihl-treaties/gciv-1949
- Core: respect and ensure respect in all circumstances; protect wounded/sick and civilians; prohibit attacks on protected persons and objects; humane treatment and fair trial guarantees.
- Caveat: applies in armed conflict; specific obligations depend on treaty and context.

## Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court

- ICC core text: https://www.icc-cpi.int/publications/core-legal-texts/rome-statute-international-criminal-court
- Consolidated PDF: https://www.icc-cpi.int/sites/default/files/2025-05/Rome-Statute-EN-2025.pdf
- OHCHR text: https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/rome-statute-international-criminal-court
- Core: jurisdiction over genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes, and aggression; individual criminal responsibility; complementarity with national courts.
- Caveat: jurisdiction is treaty-bound and limited by the Statute.

## International Court of Justice

- Statute: https://www.icj-cij.org/en/statute
- Core: settlement of legal disputes between states in accordance with international law; advisory opinions; judicial independence.
- Caveat: contentious jurisdiction generally requires state consent; not a criminal court.

## Nuremberg / International Military Tribunal

- Charter of the International Military Tribunal: https://avalon.law.yale.edu/imt/imtconst.asp
- Core: crimes against peace, war crimes, crimes against humanity; individual responsibility; official position and superior orders do not erase responsibility; fair-trial safeguards.
- Caveat: specific tribunal for European Axis major war criminals; its precedents influenced later international criminal law but are not a general code.

## General international law

- UN treaty collection: https://treaties.un.org/
- ICRC IHL database: https://ihl-databases.icrc.org/en/ihl-treaties
- Core: pacta sunt servanda, treaty obligations in good faith, state responsibility, peaceful dispute resolution, respect for peremptory norms.
- Caveat: sources and applicability vary by treaty, customary law, and jurisdiction.
